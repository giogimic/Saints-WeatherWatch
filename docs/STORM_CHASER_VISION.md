# Storm Chaser — Living Weather World

Vision document for Saints Weather Watch. Real weather stays trustworthy; gameplay layers on top.

## Core vision

Storm Chaser is a persistent multiplayer exploration game on a live weather map.

- The weather is **real**.
- Gameplay is **layered** on top.
- Players explore the region with live radar/satellite/terrain while collecting resources, researching storms, crafting equipment, and competing as storm chasers.
- Calm days still reward exploration; active weather becomes research opportunity.
- Optional **SIMULATED EVENT** content is always clearly labeled and visually distinct from official products.

## Living open world

Players drive across the full supported region (highways, towns, forests, mountains, coast, lakes, parks). Weather itself acts as the world’s changing “biome.”

## Camera

Close default zoom, smooth zoom-out for planning, pinch (mobile) / wheel (desktop), quick center, follow-vehicle and free-explore modes.

## Exploration

The map constantly generates small discoveries (scrap, wiring, batteries, journals, blueprints, scenic points, etc.) so roads stay worth driving even without storms. Rare finds feed high-tier crafting.

## Regional resources

Environments bias drops (forests → bio samples, coast → salt/ocean data, cities → electronics, farms → fuel, etc.) to encourage road trips.

## Weather research

Real phenomena (rain, snow, thunderstorms, supercells, hail, wind, coastal storms, fog, heat, cold) yield typed research materials. Quality depends on distance, gear, duration, sensors, and photography — never by inventing fake warnings.

## Simulated events

Optional, bright, stylized, always marked **SIMULATED EVENT · Gameplay Only · Not Real Weather**. Examples: energy surge, balloon failure, drone swarm recovery, lost research convoy.

## Deployables, vehicles, crafting, stations

Probes, stations, drones, vehicle labs, cosmetics, and field research networks. Crafting turns scavenged/researched materials into gear. Players can build probe networks and return later for stored research.

## Trading

A global marketplace for resources, blueprints, parts, cosmetics, and contracts — player-driven without forcing PvP.

## Progression

Advance scientific capability (radar range, inventory, craft tiers, drones, prediction) rather than combat power.

## Multiplayer

See other chasers, share/compete for the same drops and events, form convoys, trade, cooperate on probe networks. Outbreaks naturally gather players without forced interaction.

## Daily loop

Check live weather → contracts → route → scavenge → study real weather → optional simulated events → deploy gear → craft/trade → prepare for tomorrow.

## Trust boundary (non-negotiable)

| Real ops | Game layer |
|----------|------------|
| NWS alerts, radar, cams, archive | Drops, simulated events, craft, trade, XP cosmetics |
| Never altered for gameplay drama | Always labeled when simulated |

## Relation to current product

Already live: map/radar, chaser accounts, garage vehicles, XP/levels, Radar Chase loot, alert WebSocket.
