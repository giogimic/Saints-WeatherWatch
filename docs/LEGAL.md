## Not an emergency service

Saints Weather Watch is an informational and educational tool. It is **not** a
substitute for official warnings, emergency alerts, or life-safety instructions
from the National Weather Service, local emergency management, or first
responders. Always follow official guidance during severe weather.

## Data attribution

The app surfaces public weather and hazard feeds. Typical sources include:

- National Weather Service (`api.weather.gov`)
- NOAA / SPC outlooks and related products
- Iowa State IEM (radar WMS, LSR, VTEC archive)
- ODIN / ORNL power-outage estimates
- NOAA NWPS / AHPS flood gauges
- USGS Earthquake Hazards
- Map basemaps (OpenStreetMap, CARTO, Esri, and similar)

Those datasets remain subject to the providers’ terms. Saints Gaming does not
claim ownership of upstream official weather or hazard data.
