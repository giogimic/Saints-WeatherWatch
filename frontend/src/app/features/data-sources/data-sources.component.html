Data Sources & Limitations

Power Outages

The power outage data displayed on this platform is sourced from the U.S. Department of Energy's Outage Data Initiative Nationwide (ODIN). Because this data is aggregated at the federal level, there can be a delay of 15 to 60 minutes between when an outage occurs and when it appears on our maps.

For The Most Accurate Live Data

We do not directly scrape local utility websites to ensure platform stability during severe weather events. For immediate, real-time outage status, please check your local provider directly:

  • Central Maine Power (CMP) Outage Map
  • Versant Power Outage Map
  • PowerOutage.us (National Aggregator)

Weather Alerts

Severe weather alerts, watches, and warnings are pulled directly from the National Weather Service (NWS) API. These are typically near real-time, but network congestion during major events can occasionally delay updates by a few minutes. Always keep a NOAA Weather Radio on hand during severe weather.

Live Cameras

Camera feeds are sourced from a combination of public APIs, including FAA WeatherCams and OpenCCTV. Some cameras may experience dropped frames, stale imagery, or go entirely offline during severe weather due to local power loss or damaged networking equipment at the camera site.
